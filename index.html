Today's protocol

Standard: Execute the plan without negotiating with yourself. Discomfort is part of training; pain, illness or unsafe conditions are reasons to modify or stop.

The 8 attributes

Physical capacity — strength, endurance, mobility and durability.
Discomfort tolerance — stay composed while conditions are uncomfortable.
Discipline — execute commitments regardless of mood.
Stress behavior — think clearly when tired, rushed or frustrated.
Team behavior — be useful, communicate, carry your share.
Self-management — sleep, nutrition, recovery and preparation.
Competence — navigation, first aid, learning, problem-solving and communication.
Character — honesty, ownership, restraint and keeping your word.

Weekly standard

Once a week, complete a controlled assessment combining physical effort + a cognitive task + a character task. The objective is staying useful under fatigue, not destroying yourself.
No reckless sleep deprivation, dangerous cold exposure, extreme dehydration, unsafe loads or self-inflicted injury. Forge is controlled stress and repeatable performance.
