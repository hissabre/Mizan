2027 — The Big Five

    Freedom, defined

    External: not constantly working just to afford the basics.

    Social: detached from approval, comparison and people-pleasing.

    Temporal: grateful for now, learning from the past without living in it, planning the future without being consumed by it.

    Freedom isn't doing whatever impulse demands — it's having enough mastery to choose according to your principles instead of being controlled by impulse, circumstance, or other people's opinions.
